Refund Policy

Energized Empire Sdn. Bhd. · Last updated: 24 July 2026

Our Commitment

We want you to be completely satisfied with Ferbe. If you are not, we will work with you to make it right. This policy outlines when and how refunds are issued.

14-Day Free Trial

Every plan includes a 14-day free trial with no credit card required. You will never be charged without explicitly entering payment details and confirming a subscription. We encourage you to fully evaluate Ferbe during your trial before subscribing.

Monthly Subscriptions

Monthly subscriptions may be cancelled at any time. Cancellation takes effect at the end of the current billing period. We do not offer prorated refunds for partial months, except where required by applicable law.

Annual Subscriptions

Annual subscriptions cancelled within 30 days of renewal are eligible for a full refund of the most recent annual payment. After 30 days, no refund is issued for the remaining term, but you retain access until the end of the paid period.

Exceptional Circumstances

If you experience a significant service outage (more than 24 continuous hours) or a critical bug that materially impacts your use of the platform, contact us and we will assess a service credit or partial refund on a case-by-case basis.

How to Request a Refund

Email billing@ferbe.ai with your account email and reason for the refund request. We aim to respond within 2 business days. Approved refunds are processed within 5–10 business days to your original payment method.

Contact

Billing enquiries: billing@ferbe.ai
