Privacy Policy

Energized Empire Sdn. Bhd. · Last updated: 24 July 2026

1. Introduction

Ferbe is a product of Energized Empire Sdn. Bhd. (Registration No. [Reg No.], incorporated globally) ("we", "us", "our"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use the Ferbe platform at ferbe.ai ("Service").

We comply with Malaysia's Personal Data Protection Act 2010 (PDPA) and apply GDPR-equivalent principles for customers in the European Economic Area and beyond. By using Ferbe, you consent to the practices described in this policy.

2. Information We Collect

Account information: Name, email address, organisation name, job title, and password when you register.

Usage data: Pages visited, features used, timestamps, IP address, browser type, and device information.

ISMS content: Documents, evidence files, audit records, and compliance data you upload or create within the platform. This data belongs to you.

Payment information: Processed by Stripe. We never store your card details.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to: provide and improve the Service; process payments; send transactional emails (account, billing, security); provide customer support; and comply with legal obligations.

We do not sell your personal data. We do not use your ISMS content to train AI models without your explicit consent.

4. Data Storage & Security

Your data is stored on secure cloud infrastructure. We implement encryption at rest and in transit, access controls, and regular security reviews. In the event of a data breach, we will notify affected users within 72 hours where required by law.

5. Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active. Upon cancellation, your data is retained for 90 days before permanent deletion. You may request earlier deletion by contacting hello@ferbe.ai.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to: access your personal data; correct inaccurate data; request deletion; object to processing; and data portability. To exercise these rights, contact us at privacy@ferbe.ai.

7. Cookies

We use cookies for authentication, preferences, and analytics. See our Cookie Policy for details.

8. Third-Party Services

We use: Stripe (payments), Cloudflare (hosting/CDN), and analytics tools. Each operates under their own privacy policies. We only share data with these providers as necessary to deliver the Service.

9. Contact

For privacy enquiries: privacy@ferbe.ai
