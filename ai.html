Questions

Common questions about Ferbe AI

Do I need AI features to get certified?▼
No. The Starter plan covers the complete document lifecycle, evidence management, internal audit workflow, and Statement of Applicability — every mandatory ISO 27001:2022 requirement. AI accelerates the work; it is not a prerequisite for certification.
Where does Ferbe AI get its answers from?▼
Exclusively from your own ISMS data — your documents, evidence, audit findings, checklist responses, and management review records. It does not answer from general internet knowledge, which is what makes responses specific and auditable rather than generic.
Is my data used to train AI models?▼
No. Your ISMS data is never used to train models and is never shared with other organisations. It is processed to answer your queries and retained only within your own tenant.
Does Ferbe AI write my policies for me?▼
Not for you — with you. AI Assist recommends what a document should cover, which Annex A controls it must satisfy, and offers suggested phrasing that would satisfy those controls. Your named author reads the suggestion, rewrites it in your organisation's own language, and owns the result. The distinction matters: an external auditor will ask who authored a policy and whether they understood it. Text pasted in unread is difficult to defend at Stage 2 and does not demonstrate the organisational competence Clause 7.2 requires. Suggested wording is a starting point, not a finished document.
Can the AI approve documents or close findings for me?▼
No, deliberately. ISO 27001:2022 requires named human accountability for approvals, findings, and corrective actions. Ferbe AI recommends, suggests, and analyses — a named person always authors, reviews, and approves. This keeps your audit trail defensible.
Which plans include AI?▼
Growth (RM799/month) and Professional (RM1,999/month). Starter (RM299/month) includes the full ISMS platform with automated report generation and the RAG scorecard, but without the Intelligence Dashboard, Compliance Assistant, or auto-raised findings.
How is AI-generated content treated in a certification audit?▼
AI Assist produces scope recommendations, control mappings, gap flags, and suggested phrasing — all of it a starting point for a named author. The documents themselves are authored, adapted, and approved by named people, and the audit trail records exactly who did what and when. Auditors assess the document and its approval record; what matters is that a competent person owns it, which is exactly what the audit trail demonstrates.
