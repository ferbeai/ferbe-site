Cookie Policy

Energized Empire Sdn. Bhd. · Last updated: 24 July 2026

What Are Cookies

Cookies are small text files placed on your device when you visit a website. They help websites remember your preferences, keep you logged in, and understand how you use the site.

Cookies We Use

Essential cookies — Required for the platform to function. These include session authentication tokens that keep you logged in to your Ferbe account. You cannot opt out of these without losing platform functionality.

ferbe_session · Authentication · Session · Essential

Analytics cookies — Help us understand how visitors interact with ferbe.ai. We use Google Analytics 4 (anonymised IP). These cookies do not identify you personally.

_ga · Google Analytics · 2 years · Analytics
_ga_* · Google Analytics · 2 years · Analytics

Preference cookies — Remember your settings such as billing toggle preference (monthly/annual) and UI configuration.

ferbe_prefs · Preferences · 1 year · Preference

Managing Cookies

You can control cookies through your browser settings. Disabling cookies may affect your ability to use certain features of Ferbe. For analytics opt-out, visit Google Analytics Opt-out.

Cloudflare

Our platform is served via Cloudflare, which may set its own cookies for security and performance purposes. See Cloudflare Cookie Policy.
