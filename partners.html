Questions

Partner programme FAQ

Is there a cost to join the partner programme?▼
No. There is no joining fee, no annual fee, and no minimum client commitment. You earn revenue share on the clients you bring; there is no downside if a quarter is quiet.
How and when is revenue share paid?▼
Referral partners are paid quarterly; Certified partners monthly. Payment is made against invoice within 30 days of the period close, in Malaysian Ringgit or USD.
Do my clients contract with Ferbe or with my firm?▼
Either arrangement works. Clients can subscribe directly to Ferbe with your partner code attached, or you can resell under your own agreement. We will discuss which suits your practice during the discovery call.
What if a client I introduce signs up later?▼
Partner attribution is recorded when you register the opportunity, and holds for 12 months. If they subscribe within that window, the revenue share is yours regardless of how long the sales cycle takes.
Do I need to be ISO 27001 certified myself to partner?▼
Not required, though demonstrable ISO 27001 implementation or audit experience is expected for the Certified tier. Referral partners need only a relevant client base and an understanding of why organisations pursue certification.
Can I use Ferbe for my own firm's ISMS?▼
Yes. Certified and Strategic partners receive a complimentary Professional plan licence for their own organisation — useful both for managing your own compliance and for demonstrating the platform to clients.
