Common questions

Everything you need to know before you commit.

Is there really no credit card required for the trial?
+
Yes — your 14-day trial starts the moment you create an account. No card, no commitment. At the end of 14 days, choose a plan or your data is held for 30 days while you decide. We'll remind you 3 days before the trial ends.
Can I switch plans later?
+
Yes. Upgrade or downgrade at any time. Upgrades take effect immediately — you'll be charged a prorated amount for the remainder of your billing cycle. Downgrades take effect at the next renewal date.
What payment methods do you accept?
+
We accept all major credit and debit cards (Visa, Mastercard) via Stripe. For Malaysian customers, FPX online banking is available. Enterprise customers can pay via bank transfer with a purchase order.
Does Ferbe work without the AI module?
+
Absolutely — and this is a core design principle. Every document, audit, evidence, and management review feature works fully without AI. The AI module (available on Professional) is a powerful enhancement layer — it adds intelligence and interpretation on top of what's already structured, not a dependency that the platform relies on.
Is my data stored globally?
+
Ferbe is hosted on infrastructure with data residency options for global customers. We comply with Malaysia's PDPA and GDPR principles. Full details are in our Privacy Policy.
I'm an ISO 27001 consultant managing multiple clients — is there a partner plan?
+
Yes. Our consultant partner programme gives you a 15% revenue share on every client you refer or manage through Ferbe. You also get access to a multi-client dashboard, white-labelling options, and a dedicated partner account manager. Talk to us about Enterprise or download our partner deck.
What happens to my data if I cancel?
+
You can export all your documents, evidence, and audit records as PDF or ZIP at any time. After cancellation, your data is retained for 90 days — after which it is permanently deleted from our servers. We will never hold your compliance data hostage.
